# Red Wave — Hospital Blood Bank Inventory & Coordination Platform

**Red Wave** is a B2B SaaS platform and coordination tool designed for private and specialty hospitals with internal blood banks or storage units. It helps reduce blood component waste through **FEFO (First Expired, First Out)** prioritization and accelerates unit sourcing during surgical, emergency, and critical care demand—starting with pilot validation in **Faiyum**.

---

## Core Problem & Focus

Hospital blood banks manage perishable blood components under strict storage and shelf-life constraints:

| Component | Storage Conditions | Typical Shelf-Life | Operational Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Red Blood Cells (RBCs)** | `2°C` to `6°C` | Up to `42` days | Continuous surgery, ER, and ICU demand; expiry risk if batch tracking lags |
| **Platelets** | `20°C` to `24°C` with continuous agitation | Short `5`-day window | Highest waste risk due to rapid expiration |
| **Plasma (FFP)** | `-18°C` or lower | Up to `1` year | Frozen storage management and timely availability |

**Current workflow gaps:**
- Manual registers and disconnected Excel sheets delay real-time stock visibility across shifts.
- Short-shelf-life components like platelets face high expiration risk without proactive alerts.
- Sudden shortages often rely on repetitive phone calls and ad-hoc coordination rather than structured local requests.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

- **Target Organizations:** Medium-to-large private and specialty hospitals (typically `60` to `150` beds) operating internal blood banks or storage refrigerators alongside surgical, emergency (`ER`), maternity, or intensive care (`ICU`) units.
- **Primary Users:** Blood bank supervisors, lab specialists, and inventory technicians managing daily receipts, issue logs, and expiry dates.
- **Economic Buyers / Decision Makers:** Medical directors, operations directors, and hospital managers who approve pilot trials and subscriptions.
- **Out of Scope:** Retail pharmacies, standalone diagnostic labs without blood storage/transfusion units, and small clinics without surgical/emergency blood storage.

---

## Key Capabilities & Pilot Artifacts

1. **FEFO Inventory & Expiry Alerts:** Prioritizes units closest to expiration for **RBCs**, **Platelets**, and **Plasma** with early warnings.
2. **Inter-Hospital Exchange Requests:** Enables structured coordination and shortage requests among nearby facilities without relying on restricted government databases.
3. **Bilingual Interactive Web Prototype:** Demonstrates inventory views, expiry prioritization, and coordination flows.
4. **Pilot Workbook (`Red_Wave_Blood_Bank_Pilot_Workbook.xlsx`):** Tracks sample/pilot batch inventory, daily consumption rates, transaction logs, and field discovery feedback across Faiyum facilities.

---

## Business Model Overview

- **Primary Model:** Tiered **B2B SaaS** monthly/annual subscriptions for private hospitals based on facility size and feature scope.
- **One-Time Onboarding:** Setup, initial data import/alignment, and staff training.
- **Enterprise / Network Tiers:** Custom multi-site or institutional agreements (including sponsored/partnership options where applicable).
- **ROI Driver:** Lower component write-offs/waste (especially short-dated platelets) and faster emergency fulfillment.

---

## Customer Discovery & Field Validation

Field interviews in Faiyum follow **The Mom Test** principles—focusing on past behavior, real waste/shortage incidents, existing shift logs, and pilot conditions rather than hypothetical pitching.

### Quick Start for Pilot Testing
1. Review the bilingual web prototype and `Red_Wave_Blood_Bank_Pilot_Workbook.xlsx`.
2. Confirm internal blood storage and `24/7` surgical/ER activity during initial hospital visits.
3. Run a short, low-friction pilot using existing stock/sheet data while protecting hospital data privacy.
4. Log real consumption rates, expiry pain points, and decision criteria in the field notes sheet.
